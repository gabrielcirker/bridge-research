# Research archive

Original research artifacts and intermediate versions are preserved without editing their content. The current report is `../paper/Mathematical Optimization of Lightweight Bridge Structures.pdf`.

Earlier finite-difference results are superseded, not additional support for the current comparison. The original box section could not preserve equal area with a nonzero cavity inside the baseline envelope; the revised model uses a larger box and a variational beam formulation. Draft claims and citation placeholders remain as historical evidence. They are not endorsements of those claims.

The manifest records SHA-256 hashes, readable source labels and duplicate provenance. Every unique relevant PDF, LaTeX source, code file, result and figure found in the original research folders and November 3 backup folders/ZIPs is included, except an incomplete 2,890-byte local PDF which could not be opened. Its source and the readable December 2025 report are retained.

Operating-system metadata, LaTeX build logs, environment/configuration files and unrelated files are not research artifacts. Unattributed calculator sample images and 2024 CAD drawings with unverified connection to this study were not included. Referenced publications are linked, not republished.

No research source files or reports were edited for this website. The website’s main reader displays page images derived directly from the current PDF; its text alternative is extracted from that PDF.

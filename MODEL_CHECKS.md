# Model checks and limitations

The revised model uses cubic Euler–Bernoulli beam elements and integrates the variable stiffness in the bending energy. The hollow envelope is 40 by 60 mm; its wall thickness satisfies the same area as the 20 by 40 mm solid baseline. This changes the earlier geometry, which could not have a cavity and retain the full baseline area within the same envelope.

The solid beam matches the closed-form simply supported point-load deflection and Euler pinned-column buckling load. Tapered results at 40 elements are 0.688012 mm and 276.6637 kN; at 80 they are 0.688012 mm and 276.6631 kN. These checks do not establish physical validity. The model omits local buckling, material yielding, shear, imperfections, connections, and construction constraints. The reported load-to-weight ratio is an ideal axial buckling load divided by weight, not general bridge strength. This is a comparison of selected sections, not an optimization.
